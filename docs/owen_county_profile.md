# Owen County, Indiana — County Profile

> Vetted reference document. All figures carry a source and data year.
> Public data only — contains no personal information.

## Geography and government
- Owen County is located in southwestern Indiana; its county seat is Spencer. (U.S. Census Bureau)
- FIPS county code: 18119. (U.S. Census Bureau)
- The county is part of the Bloomington, Indiana Metropolitan Statistical Area. (U.S. Census Bureau)
- Incorporated towns: Spencer and Gosport. (U.S. Census Bureau)
- The county was formed in 1819 and named for Abraham Owen. (Historical record)

## Population
- Population estimate: 21,532 (U.S. Census Bureau, Population Estimates Program, July 1, 2023 — V2023).
- Population: 21,434 (U.S. Census Bureau, ACS 5-Year Estimates, 2023).
- Population: 21,321 (U.S. Census Bureau, 2020 Decennial Census).
- Owen County is the 68th most populous of Indiana's 92 counties. (U.S. Census Bureau, ACS 2023)

## Age structure
- Residents aged 65 and older: about 21.9% of the population (2024), above Indiana's 17.5% and the U.S. 18%. (U.S. Census Bureau estimates, 2024, via USAFacts)
- Residents under age 18: about 20.3% of the population (2024). (U.S. Census Bureau estimates, 2024, via USAFacts)

## Income and housing
- Median household income: $59,586 (U.S. Census Bureau, ACS 5-Year Estimates, 2023).
- Median property (home) value: $162,400 (U.S. Census Bureau, ACS 2023).
- Homeownership rate: 80.8% (U.S. Census Bureau, ACS 2023).
- Median gross rent: $912 per month (U.S. Census Bureau, ACS 2023).

## Educational attainment
- Adults holding a bachelor's degree or higher: 17.0% (U.S. Census Bureau, ACS 5-Year Estimates, 2023).

## Commuting
- Most workers drove alone to work; average commute time was about 32–35 minutes (U.S. Census Bureau, ACS 2023).
