# Owen County, Indiana — Economic Structure

> Vetted reference document. All figures carry a source and data year.
> Public data only — contains no personal information.

## Labor force and employment
- Labor force: approximately 10,368 (U.S. Census Bureau, ACS 5-Year Estimates, 2023).
- Employed residents: approximately 9,894 (U.S. Census Bureau, ACS 2023).
- Unemployment rate: 4.6% (U.S. Census Bureau, ACS 2023).
- A more recent monthly reading placed the county unemployment rate at 3.2% in December (U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Local Area Unemployment Statistics).

## Leading employment sectors (by resident employment)
- Manufacturing: approximately 2,351 workers (U.S. Census Bureau, ACS 2023).
- Health Care & Social Assistance: approximately 1,412 workers (U.S. Census Bureau, ACS 2023).
- Construction: approximately 1,112 workers (U.S. Census Bureau, ACS 2023).

## Regional wage context (Economic Growth Region 8)
Owen County is part of Indiana Economic Growth Region 8, which also includes Brown, Daviess, Greene, Lawrence, Martin, Monroe, and Orange counties. Region-level figures below are reported for EGR 8, not Owen County alone.

- Total covered jobs in EGR 8: approximately 124,696 (Indiana Dept. of Workforce Development, Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages, Q1 2025).
- Establishments in EGR 8: approximately 6,680 (Indiana DWD, QCEW, Q1 2025).
- Average weekly wage in EGR 8: approximately $1,186 (Indiana DWD, QCEW, Q1 2025).

---
*Note: Region-level QCEW figures are labeled as EGR 8 rather than Owen County, because the published series aggregates the eight-county region. Program-reported regional figures must not be presented as county-specific.*
